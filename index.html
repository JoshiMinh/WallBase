Built around your collection

From a good find to your new favorite screen.

WallBase keeps discovery, organization, and wallpaper setup together so your collection stays easy to use.

Browse in one place

02

Make a library

Save favorites, group them into albums, and find them again with filters and sorting.

Preview and crop an image before applying it to your home screen, lock screen, or both.
